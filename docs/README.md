# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview
This `docs/` folder is the single entry point for how OctoAcme runs projects across initiation, planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement. It helps new and existing teammates quickly find process guidance, expected artifacts, and collaboration patterns.

## Brief Project Management Summary
OctoAcme follows a lightweight but structured lifecycle: **Initiation**, **Planning**, **Execution**, **Release**, and **Retrospective/Continuous Improvement**. Initiation clarifies the business problem, success metrics, stakeholders, and go/no-go readiness. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimation, dependencies, milestones, and Definition of Done.

Execution emphasizes small, testable increments, visible workflow tracking, and regular team cadence (standups, weekly delivery syncs, and milestone demos). Quality is built into delivery through unit/integration testing, smoke testing for critical flows, CI checks, and security scanning. Risks and dependencies are actively managed through a risk register, recurring reviews, and clear escalation paths.

Release and deployment require readiness checks (completed acceptance criteria, passing CI/security scans, release notes, rollback plans, and verification steps), followed by stakeholder communication. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents capture what worked, what to improve, and tracked action items. This process is grounded in core principles: customer-first outcomes, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety.

Key personas collaborate throughout the lifecycle: **Project Managers** coordinate delivery, risk, and communication; **Product Managers** define outcomes and priorities; **Developers** design/build/test and surface technical risks; **QA/Testing contributors** validate quality and acceptance; and **Stakeholders** provide input and approvals.
